=== Instructions ===

Welcome To MicroMacro! We are going to make the instructions quick, so you can get on.

MicroMacro allows you to create short text macros, it does not yet allow you to control the mouse (however this is planned for future versions).

=== How To Use ===

1. Select If You're Going To Use A Quick Macro Or Smart Macro.
	Smart Macros are new and experimental (but have more options)
	Quick Macros are short text macros that repeat the same bit of text for a specified amount
2. If you've chosen quick macro put in your text, and desired amount & off you go
3. If you've chosen smart macro create a new macro (yes you can have multiple macros in macros)
	Fill in the fileds as well, it should be simple
4. Save it!	Then Run It!

=== Navigating Menus ===

Some people may find this difficult but it's simple. On the left of each option is a number, write the number on your keyboard to navigate to that menu.

The menus are based on earlier technology of what RaspberryMenus were based on. I do not recommend RaspberryMenus as it is hardly my best works.
